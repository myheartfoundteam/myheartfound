WHY WE EXIST

A place for young people to feel seen.

Mental health deserves the same care and attention as physical health. myheartfound focuses on youth mental health awareness and community support, helping start conversations that can make it easier to ask for help.

Through awareness, education, community connection, and donations, we want to help create a culture where reaching out is a sign of strength, not something to be ashamed of.
